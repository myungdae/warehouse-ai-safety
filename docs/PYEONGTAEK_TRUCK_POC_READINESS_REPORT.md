# Pyeongtaek Truck POC Readiness Report

Current gate: `NO_GO_FOR_PHYSICAL_POC`. Automated implementation does not constitute manual or field validation.

## Required progression

1. One truck only; first test parked with ignition OFF.
2. Second test parked with ignition ON.
3. Third test in a supervised controlled low-speed area.

Public-road deployment is prohibited. There is no commercial approval, operational approval, autonomous behavior or vehicle control. Human supervision is required throughout.

Actual Blink, Yawn, false-positive and Korean audibility results cannot be fabricated. They remain pending until recorded by the human reviewer. Remaining risks include camera geometry/lighting variation, glasses and face pose, talking/smile confusion, speaker environment, hardware latency and real vehicle vibration.
